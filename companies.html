Who Owns What

← Back to flash cards

There are only two kinds of company in this whole industry. Once you see that, the names stop being a jumble.

1. The movers. Three companies deliver almost everything a dental office orders — Henry Schein, Patterson, Benco. They're the trucks. They mostly don't make anything.

2. The makers. A handful of big parent companies actually build the products. Each one owns a bunch of smaller brand names.

Here's the part that trips everyone up: the brand name on the box is usually not the company that owns it. Nobel Biocare is a brand. Envista owns it. CEREC is a brand. Dentsply Sirona owns it.

So: every parent below has its own colour. Every brand it owns is that same colour. Learn seven colours instead of forty names.

The five that catch people out

Get these right and you've got the whole thing

  • Envista, not Danaher. Nobel Biocare, Ormco and KaVo Kerr used to be Danaher. Envista split off and took them. Your notes have them under both — only Envista is right now.
  • Henry Schein is on both sides. It's the #1 delivery company, but it also owns two implant makers, BioHorizons and Camlog. It's the one name that's a mover and a maker.
  • Align owns the scanner and the aligner. iTero and Invisalign are the same company. That's the whole reason they work together — and the whole reason a rep pushes iTero.
  • Dentsply Sirona pulls the same move. Primescan is their scanner, CEREC is their mill, and the scanner feeds the mill. Same play as Align, different aisle.
  • Straumann deliberately sells at every price. Straumann = expensive implants. Neodent = cheap implants. ClearCorrect = aligners. Same parent. If a dentist says "too pricey," they already own the cheaper answer.
